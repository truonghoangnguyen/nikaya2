# 2. 14 Adhikaraṇavāra

1\. Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ, āpattādhikaraṇaṁ …pe….

2\. Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ—āpattādhikaraṇaṁ.

3\. Adhikaraṇavāro niṭṭhito chaṭṭho.

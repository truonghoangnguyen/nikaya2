# 64. Sayanasikkhāpada

1.1\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū sayanagatassa dhammaṁ desenti …pe….

1.3\. “Na sayanagatassa agilānassa dhammaṁ desessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

1.4\. Na sayanagatassa agilānassa dhammo desetabbo. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca antamaso chamāyampi nipannassa sayanagatassa agilānassa dhammaṁ deseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

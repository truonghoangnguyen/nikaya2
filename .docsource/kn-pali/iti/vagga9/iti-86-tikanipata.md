# ITI 86. Tikanipāta

0\. Catutthavagga

## Dhammānudhammapaṭipannasutta

1\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ:

2\. “Dhammānudhammapaṭipannassa bhikkhuno ayamanudhammo hoti veyyākaraṇāya—dhammānudhammapaṭipannoyanti bhāsamāno dhammaññeva bhāsati no adhammaṁ, vitakkayamāno vā dhammavitakkaññeva vitakketi no adhammavitakkaṁ, tadubhayaṁ vā pana abhinivejjetvā upekkhako viharati sato sampajāno”ti.

Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati:

3\.
> “Dhammārāmo dhammarato,\
> dhammaṁ anuvicintayaṁ;\
> Dhammaṁ anussaraṁ bhikkhu,\
> saddhammā na parihāyati.

4\.
> Caraṁ vā yadi vā tiṭṭhaṁ,\
> nisinno uda vā sayaṁ;\
> Ajjhattaṁ samayaṁ cittaṁ,\
> santimevādhigacchatī”ti.

5\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

Sattamaṁ.

# THAG 1.40. Ekakanipāta

0\. Catutthavagga

## Vaḍḍhamānattheragāthā

1\.
> “Sattiyā viya omaṭṭho,\
> ḍayhamānova matthake;\
> Bhavarāgappahānāya,\
> sato bhikkhu paribbaje”ti.

… Vaḍḍhamāno thero …

vaggo catuttho.

2\. Tassuddānaṁ

> Gahvaratīriyo suppiyo,\
> sopāko ceva posiyo;\
> Sāmaññakāni kumāputto,\
> kumāputtasahāyako;\
> Gavampati tissatthero,\
> vaḍḍhamāno mahāyasoti.

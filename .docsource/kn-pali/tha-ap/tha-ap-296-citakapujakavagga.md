# THA-AP 296. Citakapūjakavagga

## Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Anuggatamhi ādicce,\
> panādo vipulo ahu;\
> Buddhaseṭṭhassa lokamhi,\
> pātubhāvo mahesino.

2\.
> Ghosamassosahaṁ tattha,\
> na ca passāmi taṁ jinaṁ;\
> Maraṇañca anuppatto,\
> buddhasaññamanussariṁ.

3\.
> Catunnavutito kappe,\
> yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

4\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

5\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā saddasaññako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

6\. Saddasaññakattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

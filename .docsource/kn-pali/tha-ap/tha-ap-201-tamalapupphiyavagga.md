# THA-AP 201. Tamālapupphiyavagga

## 9 Kiṅkaṇikapupphiyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Sumaṅgaloti nāmena,\
> sayambhū aparājito;\
> Pavanā nikkhamitvāna,\
> nagaraṁ pāvisī jino.

2\.
> Piṇḍacāraṁ caritvāna,\
> nikkhamma nagarā muni;\
> Katakiccova sambuddho,\
> so vasī vanamantare.

3\.
> Kiṅkaṇipupphaṁ paggayha,\
> buddhassa abhiropayiṁ;\
> Pasannacitto sumano,\
> sayambhussa mahesino.

4\.
> Catunnavutito kappe,\
> yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

5\.
> Chaḷāsītimhito kappe,\
> apilāsisanāmako;\
> Sattaratanasampanno,\
> cakkavattī mahabbalo.

6\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

7\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kiṅkaṇikapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

8\. Kiṅkaṇikapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.

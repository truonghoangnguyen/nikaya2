# THA-AP 370. Mandāravapupphiyavagga

## Ekacampakapupphiyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Upasanto ca sambuddho,\
> vasatī pabbatantare;\
> Ekacampakamādāya,\
> upagacchiṁ naruttamaṁ.

2\.
> Pasannacitto sumano,\
> paccekamunimuttamaṁ;\
> Ubhohatthehi paggayha,\
> pūjayiṁ aparājitaṁ.

3\.
> Pañcasaṭṭhimhito kappe,\
> yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

4\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

5\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā ekacampakapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

6\. Ekacampakapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

# THA-AP 245. Tuvaradāyakavagga

## Naḷinakesariyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Jātassarassa vemajjhe,\
> vasāmi jalakukkuṭo;\
> Addasāhaṁ devadevaṁ,\
> gacchantaṁ anilañjase.

2\.
> Tuṇḍena kesariṁ gayha,\
> vippasannena cetasā;\
> Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,\
> tissassa lokabandhuno.

3\.
> Dvenavute ito kappe,\
> yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

4\.
> Tesattatimhi kappamhi,\
> satta kesaranāmakā;\
> Sattaratanasampannā,\
> cakkavattī mahabbalā.

5\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

6\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā naḷinakesariyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

7\. Naḷinakesariyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.

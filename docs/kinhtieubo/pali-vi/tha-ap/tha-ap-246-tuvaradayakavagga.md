# THA-AP 246. Tuvaradāyakavagga

## 4 Viravapupphiyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Khīṇāsavasahassehi,\
> niyyāti lokanāyako;\
> Viravapupphamādāya,\
> buddhassa abhiropayiṁ.

2\.
> Ekanavutito kappe,\
> yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

3\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

4\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā viravapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

5\. Viravapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.

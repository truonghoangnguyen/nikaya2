# THA-AP 368. Mandāravapupphiyavagga

## Kadambapupphiyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Suvaṇṇavaṇṇaṁ sambuddhaṁ,\
> Gacchantaṁ antarāpaṇe;\
> Kañcanagghiyasaṅkāsaṁ,\
> Bāttiṁsavaralakkhaṇaṁ.

2\.
> Nisajja pāsādavare,\
> addasaṁ lokanāyakaṁ;\
> Kadambapupphaṁ paggayha,\
> vipassiṁ abhipūjayiṁ.

3\.
> Ekanavutito kappe,\
> yaṁ buddhamabhipūjayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

4\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

5\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kadambapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

6\. Kadambapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.

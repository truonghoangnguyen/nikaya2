# JA 220. Dukanipāta

0\. Bīraṇathambhavagga

## 10. Dhammadhajajātaka

1\.
> “Sukhaṁ jīvitarūposi,\
> raṭṭhā vivanamāgato;\
> So ekako rukkhamūle,\
> kapaṇo viya jhāyasi”.

2\.
> “Sukhaṁ jīvitarūposmi,\
> raṭṭhā vivanamāgato;\
> So ekako rukkhamūle,\
> kapaṇo viya jhāyāmi;\
> Sataṁ dhammaṁ anussaran”ti.

3\. Dhammadhajajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.

Bīraṇathambhavaggo sattamo.

4\. Tassuddānaṁ

5\.
> Atha bīraṇathambhavaro ca naṭo,\
> Bharurājavaruttamapuṇṇanadī;\
> Bahubhāṇi aggipavane mūsikā,\
> Sahalambatthano kapaṇena dasāti.

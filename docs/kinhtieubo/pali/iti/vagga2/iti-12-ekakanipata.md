# ITI 12. Ekakanipāta

0\. Dutiyavagga

## Kodhapariññāsutta

1\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ:

2\. “Kodhaṁ, bhikkhave, anabhijānaṁ aparijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ avirājayaṁ appajahaṁ abhabbo dukkhakkhayāya. Kodhañca kho, bhikkhave, abhijānaṁ parijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ virājayaṁ pajahaṁ bhabbo dukkhakkhayāyā”ti.

Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati:

3\.
> “Yena kodhena kuddhāse,\
> sattā gacchanti duggatiṁ;\
> Taṁ kodhaṁ sammadaññāya,\
> pajahanti vipassino;\
> Pahāya na punāyanti,\
> imaṁ lokaṁ kudācanan”ti.

4\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

Dutiyaṁ.

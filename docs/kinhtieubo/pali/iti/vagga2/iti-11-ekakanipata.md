# ITI 11. Ekakanipāta

0\. Dutiyavagga

## Mohapariññāsutta

1\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ:

2\. “Mohaṁ, bhikkhave, anabhijānaṁ aparijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ avirājayaṁ appajahaṁ abhabbo dukkhakkhayāya. Mohañca kho, bhikkhave, abhijānaṁ parijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ virājayaṁ pajahaṁ bhabbo dukkhakkhayāyā”ti.

Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati:

3\.
> “Yena mohena mūḷhāse,\
> sattā gacchanti duggatiṁ;\
> Taṁ mohaṁ sammadaññāya,\
> pajahanti vipassino;\
> Pahāya na punāyanti,\
> imaṁ lokaṁ kudācanan”ti.

4\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

Paṭhamaṁ.

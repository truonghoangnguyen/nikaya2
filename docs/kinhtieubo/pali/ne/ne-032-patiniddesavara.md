# NE 32. Paṭiniddesavāra

0\. Sampāta 13

## Sodhanahārasampāta

1\. Tattha katamo sodhano hārasampāto?

2\. “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā. Yattha ārambho suddho, so pañho visajjito bhavati. Yattha pana ārambho na suddho, na tāva so pañho visajjito bhavati.

Niyutto sodhano hārasampāto.

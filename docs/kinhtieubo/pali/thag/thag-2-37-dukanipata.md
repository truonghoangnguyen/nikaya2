# THAG 2.37. Dukanipāta

0\. Catutthavagga

## Soṇapoṭiriyattheragāthā

1\.
> “Na tāva supituṁ hoti,\
> ratti nakkhattamālinī;\
> Paṭijaggitumevesā,\
> ratti hoti vijānatā”.

2\.
> “Hatthikkhandhāvapatitaṁ,\
> kuñjaro ce anukkame;\
> Saṅgāme me mataṁ seyyo,\
> yañce jīve parājito”ti.

… Soṇo poṭiriyo thero ….

# THAG 1.70. Ekakanipāta

0\. Sattamavagga

## Puṇṇattheragāthā

1\.
> “Sīlameva idha aggaṁ,\
> paññavā pana uttamo;\
> Manussesu ca devesu,\
> sīlapaññāṇato jayan”ti.

… Puṇṇo thero …

vaggo sattamo.

2\. Tassuddānaṁ

> Vappo ca vajjiputto ca,\
> pakkho vimalakoṇḍañño;\
> Ukkhepakatavaccho ca,\
> meghiyo ekadhammiko;\
> Ekudāniyachannā ca,\
> puṇṇatthero mahabbaloti.

# THA-AP 178. Kumudavagga

## Maggadattikattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Anomadassī bhagavā,\
> dvipadindo narāsabho;\
> Diṭṭhadhammasukhatthāya,\
> abbhokāsamhi caṅkami.

2\.
> Uddhate pāde pupphāni,\
> sobhaṁ muddhani tiṭṭhare;\
> Pasannacitto sumano,\
> vanditvā pupphamokiriṁ.

3\.
> Vīsakappasahassamhi,\
> ito pañca janā ahuṁ;\
> Pupphacchadaniyā nāma,\
> cakkavattī mahabbalā.

4\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

5\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā maggadattiko thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

6\. Maggadattikattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.

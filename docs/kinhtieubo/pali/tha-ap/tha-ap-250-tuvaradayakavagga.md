# THA-AP 250. Tuvaradāyakavagga

## 8 Sattalipupphapūjakattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Satta sattalipupphāni,\
> sīse katvānahaṁ tadā;\
> Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,\
> vessabhumhi naruttame.

2\.
> Ekattiṁse ito kappe,\
> yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

3\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> …pe…\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

4\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sattalipupphapūjako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

5\. Sattalipupphapūjakattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.

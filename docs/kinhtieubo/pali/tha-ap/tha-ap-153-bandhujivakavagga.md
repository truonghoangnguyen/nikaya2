# THA-AP 153. Bandhujīvakavagga

## Bandhujīvakattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Candaṁva vimalaṁ suddhaṁ,\
> vippasannamanāvilaṁ;\
> Nandībhavaparikkhīṇaṁ,\
> tiṇṇaṁ loke visattikaṁ.

2\.
> Nibbāpayantaṁ janataṁ,\
> Tiṇṇaṁ tārayataṁ varaṁ;\
> Muniṁ vanamhi jhāyantaṁ,\
> Ekaggaṁ susamāhitaṁ.

3\.
> Bandhujīvakapupphāni,\
> lagetvā suttakenahaṁ;\
> Buddhassa abhiropayiṁ,\
> sikhino lokabandhuno.

4\.
> Ekattiṁse ito kappe,\
> yaṁ kammamakariṁ tadā;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ.

5\.
> Ito sattamake kappe,\
> manujindo mahāyaso;\
> Samantacakkhu nāmāsi,\
> cakkavattī mahabbalo.

6\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> vimokkhāpi ca aṭṭhime;\
> Chaḷabhiññā sacchikatā,\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

7\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā bandhujīvako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

8\. Bandhujīvakattherassāpadānaṁ paṭhamaṁ.

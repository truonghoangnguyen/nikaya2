# THA-AP 93. Sudhāvagga

## Sudhāpiṇḍiyattheraapadāna

1\.
> “Pūjārahe pūjayato,\
> buddhe yadi va sāvake;\
> Papañcasamatikkante,\
> tiṇṇasokapariddave.

2\.
> Te tādise pūjayato,\
> nibbute akutobhaye;\
> Na sakkā puññaṁ saṅkhātuṁ,\
> imettamapi kenaci.

3\.
> Catunnamapi dīpānaṁ,\
> issaraṁ yodha kāraye;\
> Ekissā pūjanāyetaṁ,\
> kalaṁ nāgghati soḷasiṁ.

4\.
> Siddhatthassa naraggassa,\
> cetiye phalitantare;\
> Sudhāpiṇḍo mayā dinno,\
> vippasannena cetasā.

5\.
> Catunnavutito kappe,\
> yaṁ kammamakariṁ tadā;\
> Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,\
> paṭisaṅkhārassidaṁ phalaṁ.

6\.
> Ito tiṁsatikappamhi,\
> paṭisaṅkhārasavhayā;\
> Sattaratanasampannā,\
> terasa cakkavattino.

7\.
> Paṭisambhidā catasso,\
> vimokkhāpi ca aṭṭhime;\
> Chaḷabhiññā sacchikatā,\
> kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”.

8\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sudhāpiṇḍiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

9\. Sudhāpiṇḍiyattherassāpadānaṁ paṭhamaṁ.

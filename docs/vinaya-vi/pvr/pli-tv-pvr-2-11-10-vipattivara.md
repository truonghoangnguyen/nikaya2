# 2. 10 Vipattivāra

1\. Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ vipattīnaṁ kati vipattiyo bhajanti? Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ vipattīnaṁ dve vipattiyo bhajanti— siyā sīlavipattiṁ, siyā ācāravipattiṁ …pe….

2\. Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ vipattīnaṁ kati vipattiyo bhajanti? Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ vipattīnaṁ ekaṁ vipattiṁ bhajanti— ācāravipattiṁ.

3\. Vipattivāro niṭṭhito tatiyo.

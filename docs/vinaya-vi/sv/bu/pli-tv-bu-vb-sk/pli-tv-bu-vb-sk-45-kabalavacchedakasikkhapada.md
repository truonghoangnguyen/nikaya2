# PLI-TV-BU-VB-SK 45. Kabaḷāvacchedakasikkhāpada

## Sekhiyakaṇḍa

# 45. Kabaḷāvacchedakasikkhāpada

1.1\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjanti …pe….

1.3\. “Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

1.4\. Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1.6\. Anāpatti— asañcicca, assatiyā, ajānantassa, gilānassa, khajjake, phalāphale, uttaribhaṅge, āpadāsu, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1.17\. Pañcamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

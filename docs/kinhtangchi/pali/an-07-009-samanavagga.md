# 9. Samaṇavagga

1\. “Sattannaṁ, bhikkhave, dhammānaṁ bhinnattā bhikkhu hoti. Katamesaṁ sattannaṁ? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi bhinnā hoti, vicikicchā bhinnā hoti, sīlabbataparāmāso bhinno hoti, rāgo bhinno hoti, doso bhinno hoti, moho bhinno hoti, māno bhinno hoti. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, sattannaṁ dhammānaṁ bhinnattā bhikkhu hotī”ti.

Paṭhamaṁ.

1\. “Sattannaṁ, bhikkhave, dhammānaṁ samitattā samaṇo hoti …pe….

Dutiyaṁ.

1\. “… Bāhitattā brāhmaṇo hoti …pe….

Tatiyaṁ.

1\. “… Nissutattā sottiyo hoti …pe….

Catutthaṁ.

1\. “… Nhātattā nhātako hoti …pe….

Pañcamaṁ.

1\. “… Viditattā vedagū hoti …pe….

Chaṭṭhaṁ.

1\. “… Ārakattā ariyo hoti …pe….

Sattamaṁ.

1\. “Ārakattā arahā hoti. Katamesaṁ sattannaṁ? Sakkāyadiṭṭhi ārakā hoti, vicikicchā ārakā hoti, sīlabbataparāmāso ārako hoti, rāgo ārako hoti, doso ārako hoti, moho ārako hoti, māno ārako hoti. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, sattannaṁ dhammānaṁ ārakattā arahā hotī”ti.

Aṭṭhamaṁ.

1\. “Sattime, bhikkhave, asaddhammā. Katame satta? Assaddho hoti, ahiriko hoti, anottappī hoti, appassuto hoti, kusīto hoti, muṭṭhassati hoti, duppañño hoti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta asaddhammā”ti.

Navamaṁ.

1\. “Sattime, bhikkhave, saddhammā. Katame satta? Saddho hoti, hirīmā hoti, ottappī hoti, bahussuto hoti, āraddhavīriyo hoti, satimā hoti, paññavā hoti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, satta saddhammā”ti.

Dasamaṁ.

Samaṇavaggo navamo.

Tassuddānaṁ

> 2\. Bhikkhuṁ samaṇo brāhmaṇo,\
> sottiyo ceva nhātako;\
> Vedagū ariyo arahā,\
> asaddhammā ca saddhammāti.

## CHAPTER 7

[1]:  The *Mālālankāravatthu* says that this rains was spent at Rājagaha in the Bamboo Grove, which must be wrong.

[2]:  At least two of the more abstruse discourses in the Sutta Piṭaka (M. 44 and S. 44:1) were delivered by bhikkhunīs. A number of women were singled out for special virtues (A. 1:14), and there is a collection of verses uttered by them on reaching Arahantship (Therīgāthā, Eng. trans. *Psalms of the Sisters* by Mrs. C. A. F. Rhys Davids).

[3]:  “Roses” is not literal for *atimuttaka*, but simply seemed preferable to the rather grim “gaertnera racemosa” offered by the Dictionary.

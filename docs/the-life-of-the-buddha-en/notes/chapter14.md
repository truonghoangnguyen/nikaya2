## CHAPTER 14

[1]:  The austerities described here are in the main those recommended in the Jain religion.

[2]:  The “Pure Abodes” are a part of the higher Brahma-world (*brahmaloka*) inhabited only by non-returners (see Ch. 12, p. 236), who are reborn there on their death, and live there without returning to any other world till they attain final Nibbāna.
